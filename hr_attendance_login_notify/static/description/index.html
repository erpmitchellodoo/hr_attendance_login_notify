Simple, Timely, Effective

Email Reminders

Send the first reminder after a configurable login delay and repeat it until the employee checks in.

In-App Alert + Sound

Show a sticky warning inside Odoo and play the familiar new-message sound for immediate visibility.

Schedule Aware

Skip non-working days, approved time off, flexible schedules, and employees who already checked in.

How It Works

1. Employee logs in 2. Check-in remains missing 3. Email + alert + sound

Quick Configuration

  1. Install Attendance Login Notify.
  2. Open Settings Users.
  3. Choose an internal user and open the Attendance Automation tab.
  4. Enable missing check-in emails and set the initial and repeat delays.
  5. Ensure the linked employee has a work email and a fixed working schedule.

The scheduler runs every minute. No attendance record is created or modified by this addon.
